Support

Dumbell is a focused iOS workout tracker for strength training. Log sets with scroll pickers, follow Push / Pull / Legs or custom routines, track progress, and optionally use AI analysis and an AI workout builder.

Common questions

  • Where is my data stored? Locally on your device, with optional private iCloud (CloudKit) sync across your Apple devices. We cannot see your workouts.
  • Apple Health. With permission, Dumbell can read bodyweight for calorie estimates and write completed workouts so they count toward your Exercise ring. Change this anytime in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access.
  • AI Analysis / AI Builder. Optional. Requires in-app consent. Uses a limited free allowance, then Dumbell Pro. A de-identified training summary is sent only when you use these features.
  • Subscription. Dumbell Pro is $0.99/month (or local equivalent) via Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
  • Rest timer notifications. iOS may ask for notification permission the first time a rest timer is scheduled so you can leave the app during rest.
  • Apple Watch / Live Activity. Optional companions for set completion and lock-screen rest timing. Open the watch app once after install if the companion does not connect.
  • Requirements. iPhone running a recent iOS version (see the App Store listing). iPad is not supported in v1.
