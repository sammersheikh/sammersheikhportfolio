Pick your workout

Pre-built Push, Pull, and Legs splits with exercises targeting the right muscle groups. Each workout shows which muscles you'll hit and how many exercises are included.

Track every set

Log weight and reps for each set with scroll pickers. Exercise demos show proper form. See your previous lifts right on screen so you know what to beat.

Review your history

See monthly stats at a glance — total workouts, sets completed, pounds lifted, and time spent training. Compare your performance vs last month with automatic trend indicators.

Track your gains

Muscle group breakdown shows which areas you're hitting most. Exercise progress cards highlight your improvements with +lbs badges. See your total weight increases over time.

Build custom workouts

Don't want to follow PPL? Build your own routine. Filter exercises by muscle group — Back, Chest, Shoulders, Core, and more. Select the exercises you want and start your custom workout. Or let the AI workout builder generate one for you.

AI-powered coaching

Generate custom routines with the AI workout builder, then analyze your training with AI insights. Get muscle-group breakdowns, progress charts, and actionable feedback based on your real workout history.
