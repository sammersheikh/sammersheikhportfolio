Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sammer Sheikh ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Live Lyric iOS app (the "App").

1. Data collection

Live Lyric does not collect, store on our servers, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising identifier.

2. What the app does on your device

  • Microphone. The App uses the microphone to identify the song playing nearby via Apple ShazamKit, and (on supported iOS versions) for on-device speech recognition used by live captions and unsynced lyric tracking. Microphone audio is processed on device for these features and is not recorded to a file or uploaded to our servers.
  • Lyrics lookup. When a song is identified, the App requests lyrics from publicly available sources (LRCLib and Genius) using the song title and artist. No user-identifiable information is sent with these requests.
  • Local cache and history. Lyrics are cached on your device for about 30 days for faster reloads. Your recent song history (last 20 matches) is stored only on your device. Neither is backed up to our servers or synced across devices by us.

3. Third-party services

  • Apple ShazamKit for song identification (Apple framework).
  • Apple Speech for on-device speech recognition where used.
  • LRCLib public lyrics database for time-synced lyrics.
  • Genius public website as a plain-text lyrics fallback.

Those services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

5. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact sammerahmadsheikh@outlook.com.
