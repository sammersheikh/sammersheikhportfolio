Support

Live Lyric identifies the song playing around you and shows lyrics in real time. Tap Listen, grant microphone access, and play music from any speaker, radio, or nearby phone.

Common questions

  • No match? Move closer to the speaker, lower room noise, and try again. Coverage depends on Shazam's catalog.
  • Plain lyrics only? Some songs lack time-synced LRC data. On iOS 26+, Live Lyric can still track the current line from vocals when possible.
  • Captions button missing? Live captions require iOS 26 or later.
  • Permissions. Microphone (and speech recognition on newer iOS) are required for listening, captions, and vocal line tracking.
