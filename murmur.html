Everything stays on your devices

Parakeet TDT runs locally on the Mac Neural Engine. Your audio, transcripts, and history never leave your local network.

Apple Watch dictation

Hold a pill on your Watch, whisper, release, and the text types on your Mac. Raise-to-record starts hands-free when you lift your wrist. The iPhone relays the audio when the Watch can't reach the Mac directly.

iPhone dictation & keyboard

Dictate from the Murmur iPhone app or use the custom iOS keyboard to swipe-to-text and one-tap dictate into any app. Corrections you make are remembered for next time.

Global Mac hotkey

Press the hotkey from any app, speak, and release to type the transcript wherever your cursor is.

Searchable history

Every dictation is saved with the source app, timestamp, and duration. Search past transcripts and re-type anything with one click.

Productivity stats

Total words, average WPM, dictation count, and time saved vs typing. Track your streak and view weekly summaries.

Canvas inbox

Save ideas and snippets to a categorized inbox. Canvas auto-sorts captures by tag so voice memos become organized notes.

AI transforms

Turn a rough dictation into polished text with per-transform hotkeys. Polish, prompt-engineer, or define your own rewrite.

Custom word rules

Teach Murmur your jargon, acronyms, and corrections so transcripts come out formatted the way you write.
