Murmur

Dictate anywhere.

Hold a button on your Apple Watch, whisper, and the transcript types into the frontmost Mac app. Speech-to-text runs locally on your Mac — audio never leaves your devices.

Available for Mac
⌚

From your wrist

Hold a pill on the Apple Watch, speak or whisper, release, and the text appears on your Mac. Raise-to-record lets you start hands-free.

⌨️

From your keyboard

Press the global hotkey on your Mac, dictate, and release to type the transcript into whatever app is frontmost. No clicking, no context switching.

🔒

Private by default

Parakeet TDT runs locally on the Mac Neural Engine. Your audio and transcripts stay on your own devices, not a cloud service.

📊

History & stats

A searchable dashboard shows every dictation, total words, average WPM, dictation count, and time saved vs typing it out.

Auto-discovery

The iPhone and Watch find your Mac automatically over Bonjour on the same Wi-Fi network. No manual host address, no pairing codes, no copy-paste.

Mac discovered on office-lan

iOS keyboard

A custom iOS keyboard brings Murmur's swipe-to-text and one-tap dictation to any app on your iPhone. Corrections you make are remembered for next time.
