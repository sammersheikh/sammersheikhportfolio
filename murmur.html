Everything you dictate, everywhere.

On-device transcription, a searchable history, and tools that turn your voice into formatted text in any app.

⌚

From your wrist

Hold a pill on your Apple Watch, speak or whisper, release, and the text appears on your Mac. Raise-to-record lets you start hands-free, and the iPhone relays the audio when the Watch can't reach the Mac directly.

Global Mac hotkey

Press the hotkey from any app, dictate, and release to type the transcript exactly where your cursor is. No clicking, no context switching.

Searchable history

Every dictation is saved with the source app, timestamp, and duration. Search past transcripts and re-type anything with one click.

Canvas inbox

Save ideas and snippets to a categorized inbox instead of typing them. Canvas auto-sorts captures by tag so voice memos become organized notes.

AI transforms

Turn a rough dictation into polished text with per-transform hotkeys. Polish, prompt-engineer, or define your own rewrite and replace it in place.

Custom word rules

Teach Murmur your jargon, acronyms, and corrections. Define replacements and removals so transcripts come out formatted the way you write.
