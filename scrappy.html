Scrappy
Free macOS menu-bar app

Mirror Android without the mess.

Scrappy wraps adb and scrcpy into a tiny menu-bar utility. Plug in a phone or tablet, click Mirror, and scrcpy launches for that exact device. No terminal, no memorizing serial numbers, no hunting for the right command. Wireless ADB pairing and recovery tools are one click away.

01

One-click scrcpy mirroring

Plug in any Android phone or tablet and it shows up in the menu bar in seconds. Click ▶ Mirror to launch scrcpy for that exact device, or ■ Stop to close just that session. Scrappy handles the serial number and command flags for you.

02

Wireless ADB pairing

Onboard new Android 11+ devices over Wi-Fi without opening Terminal. Scrappy prompts for the pair IP:port, the 6-digit pairing code, and then the connect IP:port. For devices that were paired before but got a new IP, Reconnect by IP skips re-pairing entirely.

03

Control every session individually

Run multiple mirrors at once and manage them independently. Stop one phone, start a tablet, refresh the device list, or kill every Scrappy-owned scrcpy process from the same menu. No more losing track of which terminal window owns which session.

04

Live menu-bar status

The icon tells you the whole story at a glance: 📴 nothing connected, 📱 devices idle, or 📲 N with a running mirror count. A 5-second refresh loop keeps the menu accurate even if you plug, unplug, or close a scrcpy window manually.

05

Recovery tools built in

When adb gets stuck, refresh the device list, restart the ADB server, reconnect by IP, or stop orphan scrcpy processes started outside Scrappy. All the usual command-line fixes are now menu items.
