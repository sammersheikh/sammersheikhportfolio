Free macOS menu-bar app

Mirror Android without the mess.

Scrappy wraps adb and scrcpy into a tiny menu-bar utility. Plug in a phone, click Mirror, and scrcpy launches for that exact device. No terminal, no serial numbers, no hunting for the right command.

01

One-click scrcpy mirroring

Plug in any Android device and it shows up in seconds. Click ► Mirror to launch scrcpy for that exact device, or ■ Stop to close just that session. Scrappy handles the serial number and command flags.

02

Wireless ADB pairing

Onboard Android 11+ devices over Wi-Fi without opening Terminal: pair IP:port, 6-digit code, connect. Previously paired devices reconnect by IP without re-pairing.

03

Control every session individually

Run multiple mirrors at once and manage them independently. Stop one phone, start a tablet, refresh the list, or kill every Scrappy-owned scrcpy process from the same menu.

04

Live menu-bar status

The icon tells the whole story: nothing connected, devices idle, or a running mirror count. A 5-second refresh loop keeps the menu accurate even when you unplug mid-session.

05

Recovery tools built in

When adb gets stuck: refresh devices, restart the ADB server, reconnect by IP, or stop orphan scrcpy processes. All the usual command-line fixes as menu items.
