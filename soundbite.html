Questions people ask.

Something missing? Write to sammerahmadsheikh@outlook.com.

Is this just text-to-speech?

No. Plain read-aloud speaks every word on the page. SoundBite first writes an AI summary of the article, then narrates that summary with word-by-word highlighting, so you get the point in a couple of minutes.

What happens to the articles I send?

The article link and its text are sent to outside services that generate the summary and the audio. Summaries are written by DeepSeek, narration runs on Modal, brief sources are found with Exa, and generated audio is stored on AWS S3. They process the data only to provide the feature, and SoundBite does not sell your personal information.

The full list is in the Privacy Policy. Please do not send content you would not want processed this way.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, in the app: Profile, then Delete account. You can also delete any article or brief at any time, which removes its audio. Account deletion signs you out everywhere straight away, and your data is purged after a 30-day grace period. Signing back in during that window cancels the deletion. Or email sammerahmadsheikh@outlook.com and it will be handled for you.

Which languages does it narrate in?

English (eight voices), Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Hindi. English narration with the default voice is free. The other voices and languages are part of SoundBite Pro. The app itself is in English.

What does it cost?

The free plan gives you 5 article summaries and 1 daily brief a week with the default voice. SoundBite Pro is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. There is no free trial right now, and the free plan is the way to try it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. You can sign in with Apple. There is no Android or web version.

Can I import my saved links from Pocket?

Not in bulk. You share or paste links one at a time, which works well for the handful you really want to get through.
