Soundbite

Listen to any article.

An iOS app that turns long reads into narrated audio. Save from anywhere, read along with time-synced captions, and catch up with personalized briefs.

Coming to the App Store

Save from anywhere

Tap the share button in Safari and send any article straight to your Soundbite library. No copy-paste, no leaving the page.

Read along

The player highlights each word as it is spoken, so you can follow along or skim ahead. Tap any sentence to jump to that moment.

Catch up with briefs

Get a curated daily rundown of the topics you care about. Each brief is summarized and narrated so you can listen on the go.

Your listening queue

Swipe through saved articles, queue up what is next, and keep track of what you have finished. Everything stays organized and searchable.

Paste a link, start listening

Drop any URL into Soundbite and the article is processed in the background. By the time you are ready, the narration is waiting in your library.

Pick your narrator

Choose from a range of natural-sounding voices to find the one that is easiest to listen to. Switch anytime without re-processing.

Easy on the eyes

Toggle dark mode for late-night listening. The whole app adapts, from the library to the player, while keeping the warm editorial feel.
